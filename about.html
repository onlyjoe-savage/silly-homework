A little about us

Furniture with feeling.

We believe the right furniture does more than fill a room. It supports your routines, welcomes your people and makes everyday life feel a little more special.

Our story

A home begins with how it feels.

Oak & Ember Interiors is a student-created furniture business concept inspired by natural materials, warm spaces and practical design. Our imagined showroom brings together furniture that balances comfort, durability and understated elegance.

We aim to make choosing furniture feel welcoming and straightforward, whether you are furnishing your first apartment or refreshing a family home.

Our mission

To offer stylish, functional furniture that helps people create comfortable and personal living spaces.

Our vision

To become a trusted furniture destination known for thoughtful design, honest service and lasting value.

Our values

Quality, integrity, creativity, customer care and responsible choices guide the way we work.
